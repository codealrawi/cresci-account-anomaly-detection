# Aggregated results reported in the manuscript

All files are aggregates (means over units/folds, bootstrap tables, run parameters); they contain no Cresci-2017 records, account identifiers or per-account scores. They are copies of the outputs of the runs described in the main README, made without modification (except where stated).

| Directory | Produced by | Content | Manuscript |
|---|---|---|---|
| `main_variant_B/` | `run_nested_cv.py` + `analyze_paired_bootstrap.py` on features of variant B (10 draws × 10 repeats × 5 folds, `run_config.json` has seed, grids, library versions) | `table_unit_level.csv` (mean over 100 units: ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, TPR@FPR 1 %/5 %, threshold metrics at q = 95 and q = 92), `table_fold_level.csv`, `table_paired_bootstrap.csv` (paired hierarchical bootstrap, Holm p for H1/H2), `table_chosen_params.csv`, `unit_metrics.csv` (metrics of each of the 100 units × methods) | Tables 2–4, 6; statistical comparisons |
| `sensitivity_variant_A/` | same, features of variant A (CR/LF kept) | the same tables; `metrics_comparison_A_B.csv` — A vs B comparison including TPR at FPR 1 % and 2 % (full ROC) | Tables 3–5 |
| `explained_variance_rule/` | `run_explained_variance_rule.py` on features of variant B | `fold_level.csv` (500 outer folds: chosen k, explained variance, metrics), `unit_level.csv` (100 units), `run_record.json` | 90 % rule: ROC-AUC 0.518, k = 7 (22 folds) / 8 (478 folds) |
| `figures/` | `make_fig01_roc.py` (from stored out-of-fold scores) | `fig01_roc_low_fpr_bw.png` — Figure 1 of the manuscript (Russian labels, black-and-white); `fig01_bw_verification.json` — numbers used to verify the plot (mean ROC-AUC, TPR at selected FPR) | Figure 1 |

Notes

* `explained_variance_rule/run_record.json`: the original contained an absolute local path of the feature file; it was replaced by a placeholder. Nothing else was changed.
* Rows of `*_unit_level.csv` are means over 100 units; values are rounded to four decimals as produced by the scripts. The manuscript reports them rounded to three decimals.
* Not included (account-level derived data or large): out-of-fold scores (`oof_draw*.npz`), `fold_metrics.csv`, `chosen_params.csv`, feature tables. They are regenerated by the commands in the main README.
* The AUC of the 90 % rule and the Holm-adjusted p-values of the two pre-registered hypotheses are in `explained_variance_rule/unit_level.csv` and `main_variant_B/table_paired_bootstrap.csv`.
