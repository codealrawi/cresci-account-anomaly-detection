## 6a. Known reproducibility limitations

* The feature extraction depends on the official archive version (SHA-256 given); results were obtained on one machine (Windows, 12 parallel jobs); numerics may differ slightly with other library versions.
* `run_nested_cv.py` hard-codes the preprocessing variant `raw` (z-score only), as in the manuscript.
* Figure 1 needs the `oof_draw*.npz` of a full main run; its fonts fall back to DejaVu Serif if Times New Roman is missing.
* Code comments mention internal stages and an earlier article (see README note).
